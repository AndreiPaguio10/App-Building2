About TechResource PH

Built for Filipino Tech Professionals, by Filipino Tech Leaders.

Founded in 2018, TechResource PH was born out of a vision to close the skills gap between Philippine IT graduates and the demands of a global technology economy. Our founding team — composed of former CIOs, senior engineers, and academic leaders — built a platform that doesn't just teach, but transforms careers.

Today, we serve over 85,000 active learners across the archipelago, with alumni placed in top firms including Accenture, Globe Telecom, UnionBank, and international tech companies.
